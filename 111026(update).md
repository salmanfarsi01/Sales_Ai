## Item 1: Deferred Concern Wording, Automated Memory Persistence & Phrasing Coverage

### 1. Statement Separation: Substantive Statement Preservation vs. Deferral Utterance
- **Root Cause**: Previously, when the prospect uttered a deferral phrase (`"ok leave it we will talk about it in our meeting Thursday at 4 pm"`), `target_concern.latest_statement` was overwritten with the deferral phrase, erasing the substantive reason (`"Still the fees. I just don't get it."`).
- **Resolution**:
  - `ObjectionRecord.latest_statement`: Strictly preserves the last substantive objection utterance (`"Still the fees. I just don't get it."`).
  - `ObjectionRecord.deferral_statement`: Dedicated field introduced to store the deferral utterance (`"ok leave it we will talk about it in our meeting Thursday at 4 pm"`).
  - Both fields persist in canonical conversation state and audit records.

### 2. Follow-Up Summary Line with Concern Detail
- `copilot/conversation_presentation.py` now formats deferred concerns with their substantive objection detail so that reps reviewing follow-up notes see the actual concern:
  ```text
  Open Concerns: 1 (commission fee — deferred to meeting: 'Still the fees. I just don't get it.')
  ```
  *(If no prior substantive statement exists, it falls back cleanly to `commission fee — deferred to meeting`).*

### 3. Automated Memory Persistence in Production (`finalize_call()`)
- **Client Question**: Is memory saved automatically at call end, or does something need to call `save_prospect_memory`?
- **Implementation**:
  - Added `finalize_call(self)` to `ConversationStateManager` (`copilot/conversation_state_manager.py`).
  - At call completion (invoked automatically at the end of `replay_dialogue_turns` and the live call teardown pipeline), `finalize_call()` automatically inspects `self.current_state.objections`.
  - Any objection that is `deferred_to_meeting: True`, `retained_for_followup: True`, or still in an active/unresolved lifecycle state is automatically persisted to `ProspectMemoryStore` with turn provenance (`source_turn_id`), `user_id`, and `prospect_id`.
  - Manual caller dispatch of `save_prospect_memory` is **no longer required** in production.
  - In test environments where `user_id` or `prospect_id` is unset, `finalize_call()` skips persistence cleanly (safe default).

### 3. Analysis of Canonical Turn 2 & Absent Decision-Maker Cap
- **Turn 2 Decision Readiness**: Prospect explicitly states *"I'm the one making this decision, no one else needs to sign off"*. Decision readiness measures at **90.0** sourced from Turn 2. Because only 1 of 4 dimensions is measured (coverage 25% < 50%), the headline score is strictly `null` and exposed as `partial: 90.0` with `active_blocker_caps: ['low_coverage']`.
- **Absent Decision-Maker Cap When Headline is Null**:
  When `coverage < 0.50`, the minimum coverage blocker suppresses the headline score to `None`. The absent decision-maker calculation still records blocker status in `active_blocker_caps` and `binding_note` for mathematical and audit integrity, but never fabricates a headline score.
- **Clear Partial Score Labeling**: UI and presentation layers label partial scores as:
  `"partial 90.0 (low coverage: 1 of 4 dimensions measured, headline unconfirmed)"` to avoid misleading reps.
- **Consumer Protection**:
  `copilot/core_intelligence_engine.py` guards against `None` readiness scores:
  - Evidence output: Formatted as `"Readiness: UNKNOWN (Insufficient Evidence)"` (never prints `"None%"`).
  - Decision model: `strategic_interpretation["readiness_score"]` evaluates to `None` without crashing.
  - Verified by automated test: `test_null_readiness_consumer_formatting_guard`.

## Item 3: Pressure Consistency, State Ceiling & Multi-Script Tables

### 1. Pressure Field vs. Strategy Alias Separation
- `push_strength`: Carries the selected pressure level (`none`, `low`, `moderate`, `high`).
- `legacy_strategy_alias`: Carries the backward-compatible strategy name (`resolve_then_ask`, `confirm_and_protect`).
- On every turn of both scripts, summary and snapshot `push_strength` consistently reflect pressure.

### 2. State Ceiling Engine
The state preliminary ceiling is **not** a static alias mapping; it is dynamically computed by the 9-stage gate safety engine in `MeetingConversionGateEngine.evaluate_push_strength` (`copilot/conversation_conversion.py:938-1221`):
1. **Hard Boundary / Decision to Stay**: Ceiling drops to `none` (`respect_record_exit`).
2. **Confirmed Milestone**: Ceiling drops to `none` (`confirm_and_protect`).
3. **Low Trust / High Threat**: Ceiling drops to `none` (`protect_and_shorten`).
4. **Conditional Reconsideration**: Ceiling capped at `low` (`explore_conditional_terms`).
5. **Logistical Friction**: Ceiling capped at `low` (`reduce_friction_reask`).
6. **Target-Blocking / Active Objection**: Ceiling capped at `moderate` (`resolve_then_ask`).
7. **Discovery / Incomplete Gate Fallback**: Ceiling set to `moderate` (`resolve_then_ask`).
8. **Gate Open + High Trust**: Ceiling expands to `high` (`direct_ask`).
9. **Gate Open + Hedged Logistics**: Ceiling capped at `moderate` (`two_window_choice`).

In Canonical turns 1–17, the gate is closed (early discovery, spouse absent in turns 10–15), producing a `moderate` ceiling. On Turn 18, explicit confirmation triggers Stage 2, dropping the ceiling to `none`.
