### 1. The Authoritative Core Rule (Spec 11 § 1)

> **"The LLM is the language generator, not the PitchProX decision engine. No implementation is acceptable in which the complete transcript and retrieved documents are simply passed to an LLM with instructions to decide what the salesperson should say next."**

Your implementation strictly honors this rule. Looking at the data flow from Turn 16 to Turn 18:
1. **Behavioral Signals** capture raw acoustic/semantic features (trust, valence, tension, agreement, commitment score).
2. **ConversationState** performs deterministic state progression, fact management, objection lifecycle transitions, and 7-condition gate evaluation.
3. **Core Intelligence** consumes the `ConversationStateSnapshot` and outputs a machine-level [`StrategicDecision`](file:///c:/dev/Sales_Ai/copilot/core_intelligence_models.py#L56) containing discrete enums (`primary_action`, `push_strength`, `what_to_protect`, `do_not_do`).
4. **The LLM Gateway** only generates natural spoken English within the explicit constraints formulated by Core Intelligence.

### 2. Deep Dive: Turn 16 Analysis (`"Mornings don't really work for us either..."`)

#### A. ConversationState Layer (Spec 09)
* **Stage**: `SCHEDULING` (Turns 15..now).
* **Gate Status**: **`CLOSED`** (Confidence: 0.40).
* **Failing Conditions**:
  1. `decision_maker_aligned = false`: The wife's presence has been disclosed (Turn 10) but not yet confirmed for the meeting. Blocker cap `absent_decision_maker` strictly caps Composite Readiness at 54% (Emotional: 59%, Logical: 50%, Logistical: 75%, Decision: 30%).
  2. `plausible_logistics = 75 (Access constraints require resolution)`: The prospect stated *"Mornings don't really work"*, which added fact `scheduling_constraint: Mornings unavailable / afternoons preferred`.
* **Client Principle #2 & #3 (Objection Decoupling)**:
  Notice that `scheduling_constraint` and `contact_preference: No daily texting` (from Turn 15) are recorded as **Active Facts**, NOT as sales objections. Active objections count remains 0.

#### B. Core Intelligence & Strategic Decision (Spec 01 & 10)
* **Strategic Interpretation**: Core detects `meeting_gate_open = False`, `decision_maker_present = False`, `boundary_state = CLEAR`.
* **Action & Push Harmony**:
  - Because the gate is `CLOSED`, Spec 01 strictly forbids `COMMITMENT_CLOSE` and `direct_ask`.
  - Core selects `push_strength = "two_window_choice"` (or `resolve_then_ask`).
  - Core sets `primary_action = CLARIFY` / `VALIDATE` (aimed at aligning the afternoon slot and confirming spouse participation).
* **Negative Guardrails (`do_not_do`)**:
  - `propose_morning_slots` (respects the logistical constraint).
  - `violating_contact_preference` / `prohibited_daily_texting` (preserves the boundary from Turn 15).
  - `treat_constraint_as_objection` (prevents unnecessary objection rebuttal loops).

1. **State & Intelligence Separation**: The boundary between **ConversationState** (the source of truth) and **Core Intelligence** (the decision-maker) is cleanly decoupled. 3. **Auditability**: Every decision is stamped with `decision_id`, `source_state_version` ($V36$ on T16, $V42$ on T18), `confidence`, and full evidence lineage.
